# Brand Voice

Governs every sentence on the site. If a line breaks these rules, rewrite it.

---

## 1. The rule

Write like Rydny talks. If he would not say it to a client over coffee, cut it. Read every line aloud. If it sounds like a brochure, it is wrong.

---

## 2. Voice

- Plain words. Short sentences next to long ones. Let the rhythm move.
- Fragments allowed. On purpose.
- Confident, never arrogant. The work does the bragging.
- Direct. Say the thing.
- Real objects over abstractions. "Marble," "odds," "Vercel." Not "solutions," "experiences," "excellence."
- Explain like the reader has zero technical knowledge. They want to know what the thing does. Tell them. Move on.

---

## 3. Conversion

- Lead with what the visitor gets, not who Rydny is.
- Specific beats grand. "Rebuilt a slow store into a fast one" wins over "passionate about web development."
- Show, do not claim. Real projects, real names.
- One action per page. One button. One email.
- Direction signals reliability. One line about where he is headed tells a client he will not disappear.

---

## 4. Beating AI detectors

**Never:**
- "Not just X, but Y" contrast pattern.
- Generic openers: "What draws me to...", "In today's fast-paced world...", "I am passionate about...", "As a dedicated..."
- Three adjectives in a row.
- Every sentence the same length.
- Every paragraph the same shape.

**Always:**
- Vary sentence length hard. Three words. Then a longer one. Then short again.
- Concrete nouns and real numbers.
- Start some sentences with And, But, or So.
- Leave one small imperfection of rhythm in. Perfect is the tell.

---

## 5. Kill list

Delete on sight: leverage, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, synergy, elevate, unlock, delve, realm, tapestry, testament, passionate, dedicated, results-driven, game-changer, empower, holistic, bespoke, journey (as metaphor), solutions (as noun), under the hood, deep dive, at the end of the day.

Words that work: build, ship, fix, fast, real, proof, done, works, hard, clear.

---

## 6. Tone

- About his work: confident and plain. What he did. What changed.
- About himself: humble. Let the projects carry the weight.
- To the client: warm and direct. Like he is already halfway into the job.

---

## 7. Formatting

No em dashes. No en dashes. Short hyphens only inside real words or for list points. Commas, periods, and colons do the rest.
